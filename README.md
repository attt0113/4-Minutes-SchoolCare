## 📊 Business Model & Financial Analysis

To ensure the long-term sustainability and scalability of the **GuardianPulse** ecosystem, we conducted a rigorous market and financial analysis based on real-world data from the Sibu district, Sarawak.

### 🎯 1. Target Market & SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
According to official statistics ([DOSM](https://open.dosm.gov.my/)), Sibu has **138 total schools** (24 Secondary, 114 Primary). Applying **Rogers' Technology Adoption Innovation Curve**, we project our 3-year market penetration as follows:

| Timeline | Adopter Category | Market Share | Target Schools (SOM) | Cumulative Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Year 1** | Innovators | 2.5% | ~3 schools | 3 |
| **Year 2** | Early Adopters | 13.5% | ~19 schools | 22 |
| **Year 3** | Early Majority | 34.0% | ~47 schools | **69 schools** |

---

### 💰 2. Cost Structure Analysis
Our cost model includes one-time upfront development costs and tiered annual operating expenses that scale with user volume.

#### 🛠️ A. Total Development Cost (One-Time)
* **Student-Level Core Development (2 Months):** RM 3,000
* **UI/UX Design & Debugging:** RM 500
* **Total:** **RM 3,500**

#### ⚙️ B. Annual Operating Cost & Incremental Maintenance
* **Cloud Database (Firebase):** RM 60/mo = RM 720/year
* **Gemini API Usage (Commercial):** RM 30/mo = RM 360/year
* **Base Maintenance:** RM 500/year (Increments by RM 500 for every additional 10 schools)

#### 📈 3-Year Total Cost Breakdown:
* **Year 1 Cost:** RM 3,500 (Dev) + RM 1,080 (Ops) + RM 500 (Maint) = **RM 5,080**
* **Year 2 Cost:** RM 1,080 (Ops) + RM 1,000 (Maint) = **RM 2,080**
* **Year 3 Cost:** RM 1,080 (Ops) + RM 2,000 (Maint) = **RM 3,080**
* **Total 3-Year Expenditure:** **RM 10,240**

---

### 🏷️ 3. Pricing Strategy & Value Metrics
To break even within 3 years across our projected 69 cumulative school customers, the mathematical baseline is:

$$\text{Price} \times 69 \ge \text{RM } 10,240 \implies \text{Price} \ge \text{RM } 148 / \text{year}$$

To ensure a healthy safety buffer and long-term financial health, we have set a highly affordable subscription price:
* **Subscription Fee:** **RM 168 / year / school**

#### 💎 Lifetime Value (LTV) & Total Potential Value
> **Customer Lifetime (Expected):** 3 Years
> **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** $3 \times \text{RM } 168 = \mathbf{\text{RM } 504}$
> **Potential Consumer Value (Year 3 SOM):** $\text{RM } 504 \times 47 = \mathbf{\text{RM } 23,688}$

---

### 📈 4. Financial Projections & Profitability Timeline

| Fiscal Year | Projected Revenue | Annual Cost | Net Profit / Loss | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Year 1** | $168 \times 3 = \text{RM } 504$ | RM 5,080 | -RM 4,576 | 🔴 Loss (R&D Phase) |
| **Year 2** | $168 \times 19 = \text{RM } 3,192$ | RM 2,080 | +RM 1,112 | 🟢 Profitable |
| **Year 3** | $168 \times 47 = \text{RM } 7,896$ | RM 3,080 | +RM 4,816 | 🟢 Highly Profitable |

* **Total Cumulative Revenue (3 Years):** RM 11,592
* **Net 3-Year Profit:** **RM 1,352**

### 📝 Conclusion
Based on our estimated total cost and a 3-year break-even target, the minimum sustainable price is around RM 148 per school per year. Therefore, the product is priced at **RM 168 per school per year** to ensure long-term commercial sustainability while remaining accessible to public institutions.
